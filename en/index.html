ePointer

Screen Annotation Tool

ePointer is a simple yet powerful virtual annotation tool that lets you draw, doodle and annotate freely on top of any application. With exclusive features such as Click-Through and the Pagination Toolbar, it lifts your expression, collaboration and interaction without ever interrupting your workflow.

Topmost Reminder

Always-on-Top Timers

Topmost Reminder brings four timer types together — Timer, Countdown, Stopwatch and Alarm — so you can time different events separately while managing and monitoring several tasks at once. Every timer can be pinned to the screen and always stays on top, so you never miss an important reminder even in the middle of focused work.
